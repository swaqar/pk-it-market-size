Thesis this page tests: the AI workloads Pakistani enterprises will actually run are mostly inference, not research training. Inference on a few hundred H100-class GPUs is within the reach of the sovereign DC build-out already underway (Sky47 13.5MW, Jazz 3MW, ~23.5MW national IT load). The constraint is not raw demand, it is converting offshore-inflow spend into onshore compute + services before the hyperscalers expand in.

08 — Cost reality for running inference in Pakistan

  • Power (dominant cost): Rs33-46/kWh (industrial to commercial, ~$0.12-0.16). 1MW of IT load at PUE 1.5 draws ~13GWh/yr, ~$1.6-2.0M/yr before generator fuel.
  • Hardware: advanced NVIDIA boards need a US export license (Tier 2, not banned); landed $40-60k/GPU. Data Vault already secured 3,000+ H100/H200. Local rental H100 ~$6/hr vs $2.60-3 for neocloud, $10 for OCI.
  • No hyperscaler region: served from UAE (<50ms) and India; the $700-800M/yr outflow funds that gap.
  • Two-sided FX squeeze: local GPUaaS pays GPU capex in USD, bills rentals in PKR, pays power in PKR. Utilisation is everything: a domestic operator must beat imported token APIs ($0.10-0.32 per M for Llama 70B, $0.22/$0.66 for DeepSeek V4-flash).
